# EOS Data Analytics


> EOS Data Analytics (EOSDA) is a global provider of satellite analytics solutions in agriculture and forestry. 


EOS Data Analytics offers a diverse portfolio of products, including EOSDA Crop Monitoring and EOSDA LandViewer. These ready-made platforms integrate extensive satellite imagery with advanced analytical tools developed by our R&D team to support data-driven decision-making across agriculture, urban planning, forestry, and other industries. In addition to tailored solutions such as crop classification, field boundary detection, and yield prediction, EOSDA can also develop custom solutions based on specific client needs not covered by the standard offerings.


## Products


- [EOSDA Crop Monitoring](https://eos.com/products/crop-monitoring/): Digital agriculture platform to monitor crop health remotely with satellite images, get comprehensive crop analytics, get notified about crop risks, and optimize resource usage.
- [EOSDA Landviewer](https://eos.com/products/landviewer/): An online platform designed to access the latest satellite imagery, visualize up-to-date satellite data, monitor and compare changes over time using a vast archive of historical images, and process multispectral and radar data directly in the browser.
- [EOSDA API Connect](https://eos.com/products/crop-monitoring/satellite-data-api/): API solution for developers to get fast and easy access to raw and processed satellite data.
- [EOSDA Carbon](https://eos.com/carbon/): This carbon solution combines satellite monitoring, machine learning, and extensive predictive data to assess soil organic carbon across large areas, enabling smarter planning, verification, and reporting of carbon sequestration.


## EOSDA Crop Monitoring


> The [EOSDA Crop Monitoring platform](https://eos.com/products/crop-monitoring/) is a satellite-based precision agriculture platform developed by EOS Data Analytics. It empowers farmers, agronomists, consultants, insurance firms, and agritech businesses with remote field monitoring through high-resolution satellite imagery and integrated analytics solutions. The platform consolidates diverse agricultural data in one dashboard — including vegetation indices (such as NDVI, NDRE, MSAVI, ReCI, NDMI, and 15+ custom indices), soil moisture, elevation maps, weather forecasts, and historical weather data. 
Apart from satellite and weather data, users benefit from actionable visual reports and recommendations like vegetation and productivity maps, risk alerts, and a field leaderboard for comparative tracking across multiple fields. As a result, users get a scalable, data‑rich platform to optimize crop performance, reduce input waste, support remote decision‑making, and enable sustainable precision agriculture globally.

## EOSDA LandViewer


> The [EOSDA LandViewer platform](https://eos.com/landviewer/) is a powerful online tool for accessing, visualizing, and analyzing satellite imagery. Users can explore both recent and historical data, apply over 20 vegetation and spectral indices, create custom band combinations, and run time series analysis to track changes. Key features include image comparison, change detection, clustering, and time-lapse animation.


Supported satellite sources include Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, Landsat 7/8/9, MODIS, and high-resolution commercial providers — along with a broader selection of other satellite datasets, offering versatile solutions for industries such as agriculture, forestry, environmental monitoring, and urban planning.


### High-resolution satellite images


> On the EOSDA LandViewer platform, users can browse and purchase premium, [high-resolution satellite imagery](https://eos.com/products/high-resolution-images/) through an integrated marketplace. Archive images date back to 2006, and on‑demand tasking delivers the latest captures at resolutions down to 0.3 m per pixel. Pricing starts from $1.70 per km², depending on resolution and sourcing options. The platform supports complex analytical workflows with previews, filtering, and instant delivery for urban planning, construction, forestry, environmental monitoring, insurance, and agriculture.


The list of the available high-resolution satellite imagery providers includes Beijing-3N, Beijing-3A, SuperView Neo-1, SuperView-2, SuperView-1, KOMPSAT-3A, KOMPSAT-3, KOMPSAT-2, Gaofen-7, Gaofen-2, Gaofen-1, Gaofen-6, TripleSat, and Ziyuan-3.

> EOSDA LandViewer serves a wide range of industries by providing satellite imagery and analytical tools tailored to support diverse operational needs and improve decision-making.


- [Forestry Monitoring Software](https://eos.com/forest-monitoring/): With EOSDA LandViewer, you can remotely assess forest health, detect deforestation, and ensure compliance with environmental regulations (including EUDR) using satellite imagery and advanced analytics.
